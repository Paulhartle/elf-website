Who we are

A community group with 40 years of experience behind it

The Ethereal Learning Foundation (ELF) is a constituted community group, established in January 2021. Our aim is to provide engaging, relevant and transformative community support that builds awareness of healthy living environments and behaviours.

In 2024 we opened the ELF Learning Provision, giving home-educated children and their families an alternative pathway to education.

Our culturally diverse team works with communities, families and young people to bring about change. Between them, our facilitators have 40 years of experience working with young people with a range of needs.

Our founder, Deb Haddon, has a background in teaching and facilitating learning, along with her own journey of personal development. She plans and runs the learning environment, bringing warmth, understanding and calm energy to build trust, safety and secure relationships.

Our philosophy

The SELF Approach™

Developed by our founder, Deb Haddon, The SELF Approach™ looks at physical, mental and emotional health, how the three work together, and how keeping them in balance helps each of us. It also explores our connection and relationships with other people.

Awareness

Of ourselves and how we feel.

Relationship

With ourselves and with others.

Understanding

Of how body, mind and emotions connect.

Connection

To other people and our community.

What it looks like in our sessions

  • Exploring who we are: self-belief, self-esteem and self-worth
  • A creative approach to the curriculum through project-based learning
  • Solution-focused problem solving
  • Core life skills: resilience, critical thinking and communication
  • A unique learning journey for every child
  • Learning how we learn (metacognition)
  • Healthy relationships with ourselves and others
  • Emotional awareness and responsive conflict resolution
  • Sustainability for ourselves, our relationships, community and planet
  • Linking learning to everyday life
  • Self-evaluation and reflection
  • Fun, engagement and enjoyment

Wednesdays and Thursdays

ELF Together

We focus on play-based and project-based learning. Activities are set up, but it is very much child-directed learning. There is usually a theme that we cover, like patterns and different countries. There is no formal teaching, but we incorporate maths, English, science and more into everything we do, with a particular focus on personal development.

We recommend coming for a visit initially, for about an hour, just to meet the staff and see the session running.
